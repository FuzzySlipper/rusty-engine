# Gaussian splat fixtures

Two tiny GLBs for the splat character tests (`docs/splat-characters.md`),
written by `rust/crates/render-wgpu/tests/support/splat_glb.rs`. They are
generated by the repository's own code and carry its licence. No character
data.

- `skinned-arm.glb`: two Gaussians skinned to `root` (green, at y = 0.5) and
  `arm` (red, at y = 1.5) of a skeleton whose arm is one metre long, under a
  non-joint `character` node.
- `arm-pack.glb`: a clip pack on the same joint names with a two-metre arm.
  Its clip `raise` holds the arm turned 90° about +z and lifts the root from
  y = 0 to y = 1 over a second.

`the_committed_splat_fixtures_are_the_writers` checks them against the
writer; `cargo test -p render-wgpu --test splats write_the_splat_fixtures --
--ignored` writes them again.
